THE GAME

PillWars is a crypto arena on Solana. It plays like Agar.io, but with real money: once $PILLY launches, every player you eat will pay you in $PILLY. Every fighter is a pill, our tribute to pump.fun, the home of the Solana memecoin scene. All you need is a wallet: fast matches, cheap Solana transactions and your winnings a click away.

HOW TO PLAY

One arcade match lasts 3:50. The arena is full of bots, and they come for you from the first second.

01
MOVE

Your pill follows the mouse. On mobile, drag your finger.

02
EAT & GROW

Eat the food dots to gain mass. The bigger you are, the slower you move.

03
HUNT

Pills smaller than you are food. Touch them to take their mass and score a kill.

04
SURVIVE

Run from bigger pills. Viruses break anything bigger than them, so hide next to one.

05
PICK SKILLS

Every 30 seconds pick one of two random skills and fire it with keys 1 to 4.
