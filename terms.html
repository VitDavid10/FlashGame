ROOMS, FEES AND PRIZES

  • Free rooms cost nothing to enter.
  • Paid rooms have an entry fee in $PILL, taken from your in-game balance. The price is shown before you join.
  • Classic: you can cash out during the match. A cashout fee applies, and it gets smaller the more kills you have in that match. The exact fee is shown before you confirm.
  • Arcade: the pot is shared among the best players of the match, after a house fee. The split is shown in the game.
  • Refunds: if you leave a paid room before the match starts, your entry fee goes back to your in-game balance. If we restart a room, every player gets back what they had at stake in it at that moment.
  • Disconnections: if you lose your connection during a match, you have a short window to rejoin without paying again. If you don't make it back in time, what you had at stake in that match is lost.
  • Results are decided by our server. If a bug or an obvious error affects a match, we may correct the result, cancel the match or return the entries.
  • Fees, prices and prize splits can change for future matches. A match you have already entered keeps the conditions shown when you joined.
