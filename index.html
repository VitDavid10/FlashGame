Where the yield comes from. Everything the house keeps goes into the reward pool: exit fees from classic rooms, the arcade cut, entries from players who disconnect and never come back, pot shares nobody claims, and the skin shop. That pool drips into stakers by the second, split by how much each one has locked. Nothing is minted — if the game earns nothing, the pool pays nothing.

Why locking for 7 days. UNSTAKE starts a 7-day wait, and those tokens stop earning the moment you ask. Without it, dropping in right before a payout and leaving right after would cost nothing, and the yield would go to whoever held nothing — paid for by whoever actually did. The wait is what makes the APR mean something.

COMPOUND vs CLAIM. COMPOUND moves what you earned into your stake, so it starts earning too. CLAIM sends it to your wallet instead. Your principal is never locked by anyone but that timer: no approvals, no permissions, and nobody — me included — can move it.
